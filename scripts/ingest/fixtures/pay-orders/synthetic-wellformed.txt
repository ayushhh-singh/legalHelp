Dated: 3rd October, 2027

OFFICE MEMORANDUM

Subject: Revision of rate of Dearness Allowance to Central Government
employees effective from 1.7.2027.

The undersigned is directed to refer to this Department's Office Memorandum
No. 1/1(i)/2026-E.ll(B) dated 22nd April, 2026 on the subject mentioned above
and to state that the President is pleased to decide that the rate of
Dearness Allowance admissible to Central Government employees shall be
enhanced from the existing rate of 60% to 63% of the Basic Pay with effect
from 1st July, 2027.

2. The additional installment of Dearness Allowance payable under this
order shall be paid in cash to all Central Government employees.

3. The contents of this Office Memorandum may also be brought to the notice
of Organisations under the administrative control of the Ministries/
Departments which have adopted the Central Government scale of pay.

(Nirmala Devi)
Deputy Secretary to the Government of lndia
